Who we are

Young people have something to say.

Youth and Masses Foundation (YaMaF) works to expand opportunities for young Sierra Leoneans to learn, lead and contribute to their communities.

Our purpose

From potential to participation.

“Youths are catalysts, not just spectators.”

A principle shared in the organization’s introductory materials.

YaMaF describes its approach as rooted in youth empowerment, educational engagement and community action. Through activities such as debates, school outreach and leadership opportunities, it seeks to equip young people to participate actively in public life.

Its work is centered in Sierra Leone, with an emphasis on community-driven solutions and responsible civic participation.

This site is currently being reviewed against the foundation’s official records. A detailed founding history and governance profile will be added after documentary confirmation.

Why we exist

The next generation deserves a platform.

Young people are not only the leaders of tomorrow. They are already shaping the decisions, conversations and communities of today.

YaMaF brings together learning, dialogue and community involvement. Our focus is on encouraging young Sierra Leoneans to express ideas, develop leadership skills and take part in building stronger communities.
